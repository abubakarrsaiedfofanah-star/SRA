About us

Built for African education, country by country.

Learn locally. Research globally. Grow practically.
Mission To give African students, teachers, lecturers, and workers a country-aware learning app for research, revision, practical skills, and AI-assisted study support.
Vision To become Africa's trusted education companion, connecting local curricula, languages, exams, universities, skills, and workplace learning in one accessible app.
Our Promise We design for real African classrooms and phones: multilingual support, country education paths, WhatsApp research, offline packs, and admin-reviewed learning content.

Country and language support
